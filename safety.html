THE ROAD SAFETY GUIDE

Be aware.
Arrive with care.

Safer journeys are a shared responsibility. Follow local road rules and the guidance of authorised staff, and give yourself time to travel attentively.

BCTWU / GAMPAHA
01 / Students ↓02 / Drivers ↓03 / Families ↓

01 / FOR STUDENTS

On foot. Fully aware.

For students

  1. Choose designated crossings and safe pedestrian routes.
  2. Stop and look in both directions. Wait until it is safe before crossing.
  3. Put your phone away and avoid headphones while crossing.
  4. Follow the instructions of authorised staff.

02 / FOR DRIVERS

Slow down. Look out.

For drivers

  1. Reduce your speed near school entrances and crossings.
  2. Watch carefully for children and other pedestrians.
  3. Keep crossings and sight lines clear when stopping.
  4. Follow road signs and the guidance of authorised personnel.

03 / FOR FAMILIES

A calm start. A safer day.

For families

  1. Choose a safe, permitted drop-off and collection point.
  2. Help children exit on the safe side, away from moving traffic.
  3. Keep school entrances and pedestrian routes clear.
  4. Plan enough time so the journey does not need to be rushed.
