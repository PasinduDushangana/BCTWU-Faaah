BCTWU / ROAD SAFETY

Pause. Look.
Cross with care.

A safer journey starts with everyday attention. Follow local road rules and the guidance of authorised staff.

↗

For students

Use designated crossings. Stop, look in both directions, and wait until it is safe. Avoid phones and headphones while crossing.

↗

For drivers

Slow down near school entrances. Watch for pedestrians, follow signs, and avoid stopping where you block crossings or visibility.

↗

For families

Choose a safe drop-off point. Encourage children to exit on the safe side and keep entrances and pedestrian routes clear.

Every journey.
Every student.
Every day.
